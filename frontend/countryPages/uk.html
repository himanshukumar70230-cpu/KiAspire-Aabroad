Overview

One year less tuition. The same degree.

British universities compress what many countries teach in two years into one intensive, research-driven year, without cutting the substance. For students weighing cost against prestige, that arithmetic is hard to beat. Add in a visa route that lets graduates work in the UK after their studies, and the one-year master's becomes less a shortcut and more a strategy.

  • 150+ universities, many ranked in the global top 100.
  • September and January intakes across most postgraduate programs.
  • Graduate visa route for post-study work experience in the UK.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Bachelor's or school transcripts
  • Academic references
  • Personal statement
  • Portfolio, for design and art programs

English Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic, typically 6.0–7.0 overall
  • UKVI-approved test where the university requires it
  • PTE Academic accepted by many universities
  • TOEFL accepted by select institutions

Financial Documentation

  • Proof of funds held for a 28-day period
  • Financial guarantee letter
  • Tuition and living cost confirmation linked to your CAS
  • Sponsor's bank statements, if applicable

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
  • Tuberculosis test certificate, where required
  • Student Route visa application
