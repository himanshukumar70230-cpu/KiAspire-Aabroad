Overview

A study permit that's built to lead somewhere.

Canada's international education system is designed with a second question in mind — not just where will you study, but what happens after. Post-Graduation Work Permits, in-demand-field pathways, and a genuinely multicultural job market mean the degree is often just the first step. We build your university list and your permit application around that fuller picture.

  • Public university tuition well below US and UK equivalents.
  • Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) of up to 3 years for eligible master's programs.
  • Consistently ranked among the safest countries for international students.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Transcripts with grading-scale conversion
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Standardised test scores, where required

English Proficiency

  • IELTS General or Academic
  • TOEFL iBT
  • PTE Academic
  • Duolingo English Test, accepted by select institutions

Financial Documentation

  • Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC)
  • Proof of tuition payment
  • Bank statements covering a 12-month history
  • Sponsor documentation, if applicable

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Letter of Acceptance (LOA)
  • Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)
  • Valid passport
  • Study permit application, including biometrics
