Overview

Ranked globally. Built for arrivals.

Australian universities take international enrolment seriously, from dedicated support offices to campuses genuinely built around a global student body. Combined with strong rankings across engineering, health sciences, and business, and a visa system that allows paid work throughout your studies, it's an easier transition than most students expect.

  • 8 Australian universities rank in the world's top 100.
  • Up to 48 hours of paid work per fortnight while classes are in session.
  • Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) for post-study work rights.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Academic transcripts
  • Statement of purpose supporting the Genuine Student criterion
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Portfolio or work samples, for select programs

English Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic, 6.0 overall for most Subclass 500 programs
  • PTE Academic accepted by every university
  • TOEFL iBT accepted by most institutions
  • Cambridge English, accepted by select universities

Financial Documentation

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
  • Proof of funds, currently AUD 29,710 for one year
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
  • Sponsor documentation, if applicable

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • Genuine Student (GS) statement
  • Subclass 500 visa application
  • Health examination, where required
