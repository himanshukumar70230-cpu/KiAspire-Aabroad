Overview

The tuition is free. The degree still isn't easy.

Germany funds higher education as public infrastructure — most public universities charge next to nothing in tuition, even for international students, while still producing some of the world's strongest engineering, automotive, and research programs. The trade-off is real: German academic culture expects independence and, for many programs, at least conversational German. Students who go in prepared for that tend to come out with both a strong degree and a genuine shot at staying on to work.

  • Public university tuition is free or near-free in most German states.
  • India is now the single largest source country for international students in Germany.
  • An 18-month residence permit to search for qualifying work after graduation.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Academic transcripts
  • APS Certificate verifying Indian academic documents
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation

Language Proficiency

  • IELTS or TOEFL for English-taught programs
  • German language certificate (TestDaF/DSH) for German-taught programs
  • PhD and Erasmus Mundus applicants are typically exempt
  • Foundation year (Studienkolleg) option for gaps

Financial Documentation

  • Blocked account (Sperrkonto) confirmation, currently €11,904
  • Proof of health insurance
  • Sponsor documentation, if applicable
  • Semester contribution payment receipt

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • University admission letter
  • Consular Services Portal (CSP) application
  • Biometrics appointment via VFS Global
