Overview

A small country, an outsized education.

Singapore punches well above its size in higher education — NUS and NTU sit consistently among Asia's top-ranked universities, instruction is entirely in English, and the city itself doubles as a base camp for regional employers. For students who want rigour without leaving the region, it's a compelling middle ground between staying close to home and going fully Aabroad.

  • NUS and NTU rank among the world's top 30 universities.
  • English-medium instruction across every public university.
  • Regional headquarters of major multinational firms based in Singapore.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Academic transcripts
  • Standardised test scores, where required
  • Personal statement
  • Letters of recommendation

English Proficiency

  • IELTS or TOEFL, waived for many Indian curricula boards
  • SAT or ACT for select undergraduate programs
  • University-specific English assessment
  • Qualification recognition check against MOE standards

Financial Documentation

  • Proof of funds for tuition and living costs
  • Bank guarantee, required by some institutions
  • Sponsor's financial documents
  • Scholarship or bursary confirmation, if applicable

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter
  • Student's Pass application via the IStudent e-Service
  • Disembarkation card completed on arrival
