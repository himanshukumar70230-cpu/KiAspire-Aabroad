Overview

The same degree. A shorter flight.

Dubai's education free zones host branch campuses of universities from the UK, Australia, and India, meaning students earn the same accredited degree without the same distance from home. Add tax-free earnings, a multicultural workplace, and a flight time under four hours from most Indian cities, and Dubai becomes less a compromise than a genuinely strategic choice.

  • Branch campuses of UK, Australian, and Indian universities in Dubai.
  • No personal income tax on earnings after graduation.
  • Under 4 hours flight time from most major Indian cities.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Attested academic transcripts
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation
  • Equivalency certificate, if required by the awarding body

English Proficiency

  • IELTS or TOEFL, often waived for English-medium applicants
  • University-specific placement test
  • EmSAT accepted by some federal universities
  • Foundation year option if requirements aren't met

Financial Documentation

  • Proof of tuition payment or sponsorship
  • Recent bank statements
  • Scholarship confirmation, if applicable
  • Health insurance proof

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Passport valid 6+ months
  • University-sponsored student visa application
  • Medical fitness test and Emirates ID processing
  • Attested certificates (MOFA attestation)
