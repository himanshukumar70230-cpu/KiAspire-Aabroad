Overview

America doesn't have one education system. It has thousands.

The United States is home to more accredited universities than any other country, from small liberal arts colleges to research powerhouses that shape entire industries. That range is the advantage: whatever you want to study, there's likely a program built specifically for it. The challenge is navigating that scale without getting lost in rankings alone.

  • 4,000+ accredited colleges and universities across 50 states.
  • Flexible major systems that let you specialise later, not on day one.
  • Optional Practical Training (OPT) extends your stay to work after graduation.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • High school or bachelor's transcripts
  • SAT/ACT for undergraduate, GRE/GMAT for select graduate programs
  • Statement of purpose
  • 2–3 letters of recommendation

English Proficiency

  • TOEFL iBT, typically 80+
  • IELTS, typically 6.5+
  • Duolingo English Test, accepted by many universities
  • Waivers possible for prior English-medium education

Financial Documentation

  • Bank statements showing sufficient funds
  • Affidavit of financial support, if sponsored
  • Form I-20 issued by the university
  • Scholarship or assistantship confirmation, if applicable

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • SEVIS fee payment receipt
  • Visa interview appointment at a US consulate
