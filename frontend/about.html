Our Story

We got tired of watching good students get bad advice.

Before KiAspire Aabroad existed, our founders spent years inside the industry watching the same pattern repeat: students pushed toward whichever university was easiest to sell, essays written by someone who'd never spoken to the applicant, and visa files assembled the night before the deadline. We built this consultancy as the alternative — one counsellor per student, a shortlist built around fit and funding rather than referral fees, and a visa strategy that starts on day one instead of week twelve.

  • Founded in Kolkata in 2012, still independently owned.
  • 500+ students placed across 18 countries.
  • Independent advice — no hidden incentives shaping your shortlist.
